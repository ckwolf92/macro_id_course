# Identification in Macroeconomics

## Overview

This is a class in identification in macroeconomics, covering empirical methods and their relation to structural macroeconomic modeling, with a focus on business cycles. The key overarching objective is to teach students how to combine data and modeling structure to think about the classical questions of business-cycle analysis, from the origins of business cycles to the efficacy and desirability of stabilization policy.

The current materials -- syllabus, slides, and exercises -- are for the Fall 2026 version of the class, taught at MIT.
